⚔️ 1. How to Answer When Civil Law Authors Disagree ("The Bulletproof Two-View ALAC")

Whenever Tolentino, Paras, Balane, and Timoteo Aquino clash, use the Supreme Court En Banc / Aquino 3rd Ed. rule first, and add one sentence at the end noting the alternative view so your professor gives you 100% full credit either way!

Controversial Topic Old / Minority View 🏆 Winning Bar & Aquino 3rd Ed. Answer
1. Art. 992 "Iron Curtain Rule" (Grandchild representing parent in grandparent's estate) Diaz v. IAC / Old Tolentino: Absolute barrier between legitimate & illegitimate relatives. Aquino v. Aquino (Dec. 7, 2021 En Banc): Grandchildren (legitimate or illegitimate) CAN represent their predeceased parent in the grandparent's estate under Art. 982!
2. 1 Legitimate Child + Surviving Spouse in Intestacy (Art. 996 vs. Art. 892) Literal view: Art. 996 says "children" (plural), so give 3/4 to child and 1/4 to spouse. Santillon v. Miranda: 1/2 to the Child, 1/2 to the Surviving Spouse in intestacy (1/4 in Art. 892 is only the minimum testate legitime).
3. Filiation of Child Born BEFORE a Void Art. 36 Marriage (2026 Bar Q#3) Literal reading of Art. 54 FC ("conceived or born before judgment"). Illegitimate (Arts. 165, 177–178 FC): Art. 54 applies to children conceived/born inside the void Art. 36 marriage; a child born before the marriage cannot be legitimated by a void marriage! Mother has sole custody (Art. 176/213).
4. Institution of Compulsory Heirs + Stranger in Equal Shares (Art. 846 & 2026 Bar Q#20) Merged-Share View: Divide whole estate equally first, since share >= legitime. Aquino / Separate-Legitime View: Satisfy the compulsory heirs' strict legitime (1/2) first, then divide the 1/2 Free Portion equally among the instituted heirs! (Show both computations in essay!).
5. Reserva Troncal (Art. 891) — Who wins among 3rd-degree reservatarios? Scaevola: All 3rd-degree relatives inherit equally per capita. Manresa / SC (Florentino; Padura): Regular rules of intestate succession apply within the 3rd-degree class (nephews/nieces exclude uncles/aunts; full-blood 2:1 over half-blood).

🏛️ 2. High-Yield Civil Law Review Master Tables (2026 Bar Syllabus)

Doctrine / Law ⚡ 5-Second Golden Rule for the Exam
Four Defective Contracts
(Arts. 1381, 1390, 1403, 1409)		 • Rescissible (Art. 1381): Economic lesion (>1/4 for ward/absentee) or fraud of creditors (4 yrs).
• Voidable (Art. 1390): 1 minor/incapacitated party or vitiated consent (M-V-I-U-F) (4 yrs).
• Unenforceable (Art. 1403): Unauthorized agent; Statute of Frauds (executory only!); or BOTH parties are minors (2026 Bar Q#7).
• Void (Art. 1409): Illicit object/cause, simulated, missing essential element — Imprescriptible (Art. 1410).
Builder in Good Faith (Art. 448) vs. Lessee (Art. 1678)
(2026 Bar Q#15)		 • Builder in GF (Art. 448): Believes he owns the land. Landowner chooses: (1) Appropriate + full indemnity (builder has right of retention rent-free), OR (2) Sell land to builder (unless land is considerably more valuable).
• Lessee (Art. 1678): NEVER a builder in GF! Lessor chooses to pay ONLY 1/2 of useful improvements at lease end (or let lessee remove them). NO right of retention!
Recto Law (Art. 1484) vs. Maceda Law (R.A. 6552)
(2026 Bar Q#13)		 • Recto Law (Movables on Installment): (1) Specific Performance, (2) Cancel (if 2+ installments unpaid), (3) Foreclose Chattel Mortgage (if 2+ unpaid — no deficiency recovery). Trap: Execution sale of the car under Remedy #1 (Specific Performance) is NOT foreclosure, so deficiency CAN be recovered!
• Maceda Law (Residential Realty on Installment, ≥2 yrs paid): Grace period = 1 mo/yr paid. CSV = 50% + 5%/yr after Yr 5 (max 90%). Cancellation requires Notarial Notice + Actual CSV Payment + 30 days.
Co-Ownership of Partners: Art. 147 vs. Art. 148 FC • Art. 147 (Capacitated to Marry Each Other): Equal 50/50 presumption; care and maintenance of family/household counts as joint contribution!
• Art. 148 (Incapacitated / Bigamous / Adulterous): Must prove ACTUAL contribution of money, property, or industry (household care = PHP 0!).
Land Titles: R.A. 11573 & Assurance Fund
(2026 Bar Q#10)		 • R.A. 11573 (Pasig Rizal 2022 En Banc): Shortened possession of A&D land from June 12, 1945 to 20 YEARS immediately preceding application; applies retroactively!
• Assurance Fund (Sec. 95, P.D. 1529): Requires GOOD FAITH. Buying after a Notice of Lis Pendens is annotated = BAD FAITH (no Assurance Fund recovery!).
